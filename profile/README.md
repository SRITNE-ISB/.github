## Srini Raju Centre for IT and the Networked Economy

A research centre at the Indian School of Business. We study how digital
technology reshapes firms, markets and public systems, and we build the tools
our research and teaching need.

This organisation holds the code behind that work.

### Current work

**SRITNE Agent Platform** — shared infrastructure for the LLM applications we
run: a sensitivity gateway that classifies and redacts requests before they
leave our control, model routing, knowledge retrieval, and a feedback store we
own. New agents build on it rather than reimplement it.

**Growth Intelligence Platform** — the first application on that platform.
Growth Audit, GTM Canvas, benchmarks and diagnostic tools.

Research tooling (survey builders, interview and enumerator assistants) and
data assets from our field work follow the same pattern.

### Notes

Most repositories here are built for a specific study or course and are opened
up when they are useful beyond it. Repository-level `README` and
`CONTRIBUTING` files are the authority on how to run and contribute to each
one.

For collaborations and research partnerships, contact the centre through
[isb.edu](https://www.isb.edu).
