## About us

SRITNE studies how digital technology reshapes firms, markets and public systems — and turns that research into tools people can actually use. This GitHub organisation hosts the engineering side of that work: the platforms, agents and datasets behind our research and academic programmes.

Our operating principle is to **build institutional assets, not one-off projects**. Every repository here is meant to be reused by the next study, the next course, and the next product.

## What we build

| Area | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **SRITNE Agent Platform** | Shared infrastructure — privacy and sensitivity gateway, tiered model routing, knowledge retrieval, and a feedback & evaluation store — that every SRITNE agent plugs into instead of rebuilding. |
| **Growth Intelligence Platform** | The platform's first tenant: Growth Audit, GTM Canvas, benchmarks and diagnostic tools for founders and students. |
| **Research tooling** | Survey builders, interview copilots and enumerator assistants supporting field and lab research. |
| **Datasets & benchmarks** | Curated, governed data assets from our research and teaching workflows. |

## How we work

- **Confidentiality by construction.** Requests are classified and redacted before anything reaches an external model, so sensitive data stays under SRITNE's control by default.
- **Cost-efficient scale.** Routine work is routed to smaller models; the expensive ones are reserved for the small share of requests that genuinely need them.
- **An in-house learning loop.** Interactions, ratings and outcomes are captured in a SRITNE-owned store, so the intelligence generated by our users compounds inside the institution.
